Buwan ng Wika

Buwan ng Wika is celebrated every August in the Philippines with cultural activities, traditional costumes, folk dances, and language-centered contests to honor the Filipino language and heritage. Buwan ng Wika, or National Language Month, is observed throughout August to promote the Filipino language and celebrate the country’s cultural identity. It originated as Linggo ng Wika in 1946, initially held from March 27 to April 2 to honor the birthday of Tagalog poet Francisco Balagtas, and later moved to August 13–19 to coincide with President Manuel L. Quezon’s birthday. In 1997, it was expanded to cover the entire month of August under Proclamation No. 1041
